# Hyperframes Composition Brief: ProjectsByAxy (Phonk / Dark Funk Edit)

## Objective
A ~25.4s beat-locked montage edit of ProjectsByAxy to a user-supplied phonk/dark-funk track (`akxneshiva.mp3`, 200 BPM), full site tour, chaotic tone.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-2026-09-24-184108/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output-2026-09-24-184108/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080, Duration: ~25.357s

## Beat data
`beats/assets/music/akxneshiva.mp3.json` — 231 detected beats, 200 BPM. Every scene-cut timestamp in `brag-plan.md` is a real strong-beat time from this file.

## Source Material
Same real site content as the prior cinematic full-tour cut (mission statement, wordmark, 6 services, about stats, 4 process phases, 3 project titles, 3 pricing tiers, 1 testimonial quote, real Intake Terminal + success state). The real hero video (`assets/video/hero.mp4`, already sourced for the cinematic cut) is reused as the drop-moment backdrop.

## Creative Direction
- Tone: `chaotic`, phonk/montagem-specific — zoom-punch entrances (scale ~1.15→1.0), flash-cut transitions between every scene, glitch/static accent on the cold open and the drop, darker/grittier grade than the cinematic cut.
- Every cut is beat-locked — this is the difference between an "edit" and a fast slideshow.

## Hyperframes Instructions
Load `hyperframes-core`, `hyperframes-animation`, `hyperframes-cli`. Run `npx hyperframes check` before render.
