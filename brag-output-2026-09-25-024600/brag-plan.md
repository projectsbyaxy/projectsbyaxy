# Brag Plan: The Portfolio Playbook (PDF Lead Magnet)

## What is this app?
A free 7-page PDF guide ("The Portfolio Playbook") that ProjectsByAxy just shipped — it teaches small business owners how to build a converting portfolio site, includes a copy-paste "Skeleton Prompt" for AI tools, and ends with a soft CTA back to ProjectsByAxy. It's a lead magnet for Instagram.

## The angle
This isn't a website launch, it's a document drop — so the video should feel like a cinematic reveal of a physical object: pages gliding past camera, the accent orange cutting through black, the "Skeleton Prompt" page held long enough to register as the real deliverable (that's the thing people actually want), closing on the free/CTA beat.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Hard black. The cover page's kicker "AX-DOC // 001 · FREE RESOURCE" ignites in orange, then the full cover slams in with the orange glow.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The "anatomy of a portfolio" page (page 3) — the numbered section breakdown, orange left-rules.
- The Skeleton Prompt page (page 5) — the boxed monospace prompt, this is the payoff of the whole guide.
- The "6 mistakes" page (page 6) — quick beat, the × marks give it rhythm.

## Outro / punchline
CTA page (page 7) — "WE BUILD THESE FOR A LIVING." holds, then cut to black on the final hit.

## User flow worth showing
none — this is a document, not an app. Centerpiece is paging through the real rendered PDF content itself (real screenshots of each guide page, not recreated text).

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: same phonk/dark-funk edit language as the site launch video — real footage (real page renders), minimal captions, beat-cut, no synthetic text-card slides
- Interpretation: wide holds on each page long enough to register as a real document, Ken Burns drift (not spin/skew), cuts land on strong beats, captions are small one-line corner labels, never full-bleed text over the page image

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 18s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #000000
- Accent: #fb923c
- Text: #f5f0e8
- Display font: Archivo Black
- Body font: JetBrains Mono
- Strongest visual element: the boxed "Skeleton Prompt" page — orange border, orange bracket placeholders inside a monospace block

## Share copy (draft)
Free download: the exact playbook (and AI prompt) we used to build ProjectsByAxy — link in bio.

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, matches the existing site launch video's edit
- Music: assets/music/akxneshiva.mp3 (same track as the site launch video), ~200 BPM, beat grid pre-detected in beats/assets/music/akxneshiva.mp3.json (first strong beat 1.357s)
- Music treatment: start at 0, full volume throughout (it's already the hook), hard fade on final cut to black
- Music cue guidance: cold open lands on beat 1.357s (strength 0.872); page cuts snap to beat grid, favoring beats with strength > 0.5 for hard cuts and weaker beats for holds; restraint on the Skeleton Prompt page — let it breathe for ~1.5s off-beat since it's the payoff, don't cut it away on the very next beat
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — page glow/vignette may pulse gently with beat strength on the cover and outro only
- SFX posture: sparse, matching prior video's discipline — one hit on cold open, one on the Skeleton Prompt reveal, one on outro. No flash/shake effects layered on top of the readable page content (same lesson learned on the intake-form sequence in the site video: content must stay legible, no strobing over text-heavy shots)
- Audio-coupled moments: page-cut snaps to beat; Skeleton Prompt page arrival gets one soft impact
- Restraint rule: never flash or shake while a text-heavy page (anatomy, prompt, mistakes) is held on screen — only the cold-open and outro get the harder hit treatment
