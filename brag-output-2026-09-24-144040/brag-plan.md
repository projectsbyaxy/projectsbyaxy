# Brag Plan: ProjectsByAxy (Cinematic — Full Site Tour)

## What is this app?
ProjectsByAxy is a cyberpunk-styled AI creative agency site for Indian founders — brand, web, D2C, content, video, and growth marketing — that talks about itself like a black-ops tech briefing, down to treating a contact form submission as a "secure transmission."

## The angle
This cut covers the whole site, not just the hero and one centerpiece — every real section (Services, About, Process, Projects, Pricing, Testimonials, Contact) gets one full-bleed cinematic beat. The through-line is the site's own mission statement, which is the most trailer-ready line on the page: "We don't build websites. We engineer digital ecosystems that shift market realities."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black frame. The mission statement splits across two full-bleed beats: "WE DON'T BUILD WEBSITES." then "WE ENGINEER DIGITAL ECOSYSTEMS." — both pulled verbatim from the real About section.

## Key moments (the middle)
One cinematic beat per real site section, each a single full-bleed idea:
- Services — the six real service names, fast-building list.
- About — "Hyderabad. India-Built. World-Deployed." plus the real stat row (25+ Projects, 20+ Clients, 98% Satisfaction).
- Process — the four real pipeline phases (Scan → Architect → Deploy → Sync).
- Projects — the three real portfolio titles (Bespoke Web Systems, Predictive Ops, Auto-Content).
- Pricing — the three real tier names (Starter → Growth → Domination).
- Testimonials — one real (fictional-client) quote flash.
- Contact — the real Intake Terminal, condensed to its dramatic essentials: card, press, success.

## Outro / punchline
"PROJECTSBYAXY" slams full-screen. "SHIFT YOUR REALITY." underneath. Long hold.

## User flow worth showing
The Intake Terminal, compressed to its dramatic core (same treatment as the previous cinematic cut): the card materializes with fields already populated, the cursor presses `TRANSMIT REQUEST →`, hard cut to the real success state (`REQUEST TRANSMITTED`).

## Tone
- Preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: a trailer-scale tour of the entire site, not just the hero — the mission statement's own "digital ecosystems" language earns the epic treatment.
- Interpretation: **this run trades the tone's usual "comfortable" scene length for "one idea, tightly held" pacing** — covering all 8 real sections inside the hard 15-25s ceiling means each beat gets ~2-3.5s instead of cinematic's default 3-5s. Full-bleed, single-focus, ALL CAPS/heavy display type, and dramatic wipe/crossfade transitions are preserved; only the hold duration compresses. This is a deliberate trade-off made explicit here rather than either blowing past 25s or dropping sections the user asked to include.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background `#000000`, text `#ffffff` / reduced-opacity white, accent `#fb923c` (orange-400)
- Display font: Archivo Black (bundled Hyperframes font, stands in for the site's Space Grotesk)
- Body/label font: JetBrains Mono (bundled, exact match to the site)
- Strongest visual element: the real Intake Terminal UI and its "REQUEST TRANSMITTED" success state

## Share copy (draft)
"We don't build websites. We engineer digital ecosystems." A full tour of ProjectsByAxy — brand, web, AI content, and a contact form that reads like a secure transmission. ⚡ projectsbyaxy.com

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — restrained under the quick beats, swelling only at the two biggest reveals (wordmark, outro)
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (only bundled option; same pragmatic restrained-volume-arc approach as the previous cinematic cut, since the track itself is upbeat business-pop, not orchestral)
- Music treatment: low under the hook (~0.15), swell into the wordmark reveal (~0.75), settle to a steady ~0.5 through the eight-section tour so the rapid pace doesn't fight the track, dip low under the terminal's dramatic hold, swell to ~0.8 for the outro slam, fade over the final hold
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse, weighty, one hit per major reveal only — never per-section (with 8 sections in ~16s, per-section SFX would read as chattery, breaking the cinematic restraint)
- Audio-coupled moments: soft impact on each hook line, deeper impact on the wordmark, a single weighty hit on the terminal button press, a confirmation tone on the success checkmark, a low resonant hit on the outro slam
- Restraint rule: no SFX on the Services/About/Process/Projects/Pricing/Testimonials beats themselves — let the music and the crossfade cuts carry that stretch, so the two real interaction moments (button press, success) keep their weight
