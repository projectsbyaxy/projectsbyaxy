# Brag Plan: ProjectsByAxy (True Motion Graphics Redo)

## What went wrong last time
The previous cut ("cinematic motion-graphics") was a slideshow: real site screenshots faded in once, sat static for ~2s, with a couple of triangle accents. That is not motion graphics — the user called it correctly. Real motion graphics means graphic elements that move continuously throughout a scene's duration, not entrance-animation-then-hold.

## The angle
A full rebuild, illustrated motion graphics only — no real site screenshots this time. Every scene has continuous, ongoing animation for its entire duration: an orbiting dot on a ring, a traveling energy pulse along a connector line, a moving progress marker, a slowly spinning accent ring. The real hero video is kept (it is footage, not a static screenshot, and was an explicit prior requirement to show the founder's real branding) — everything else becomes abstract/illustrated in ProjectsByAxy's black/orange/Archivo Black+JetBrains Mono identity. All on-screen claims are pulled verbatim from the site's real copy (hero line, service names, process step names, tagline) — no invented stats this time (the "+43%" in the previous cut was fabricated, which was a mistake).

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cold open mark, then kinetic type using the site's real hero line: small mono "WE DON'T BUILD" over huge orange Archivo Black "WEBSITES." — with a small ring of shards continuously orbiting the type for the whole scene.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Real hero video drop (kept, unchanged asset) — now with a slowly spinning dashed ring behind the wordmark for continuous motion.
- New: an animated node network (continuous traveling light pulses along connector lines, continuously pulsing nodes) captioned with the site's real second hero line, "We Engineer Digital Ecosystems."
- New: a services ring — the 6 real service names (Brand Identity, Web Development, D2C Store Development, AI Content Creation, Video & Reels, Growth Marketing) fixed around a continuously spinning dashed ring with an orbiting marker dot chasing around it.
- New: a process stepper — the 4 real process step names (Discovery & Neural Mapping, System Design & Blueprint, High-Velocity Build, Optimization & Handoff) with a highlight dot continuously traveling left to right along the track, lighting up each step as it passes.
- Punchline kinetic type, same orbiting-shard technique as the hook.

## Outro / punchline
Logo + the site's real tagline ("Shift Your Reality.") with a continuously spinning ring accent, hard cut to black.
