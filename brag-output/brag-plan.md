# Brag Plan: ProjectsByAxy

## What is this app?
ProjectsByAxy is a cyberpunk-styled AI creative agency site for Indian founders — brand, web, D2C, content, video, and growth marketing — where even the contact form is dressed up as a "secure transmission protocol."

## The angle
This isn't a normal agency site. It talks about itself like a black-ops tech briefing: "neural logic," "high-velocity delivery," "AX-PROTOCOL." The joke and the flex are the same thing — a website that treats submitting a contact form like initiating a secure data transmission. Lean into that self-serious hacker-terminal energy and let it read as confident, not silly.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black frame. A thin orange scanline sweeps once. The `AX // PBA` mark types in character by character with soft key-tick sound, then the full headline `PROJECTSBYAXY // AI SOLUTIONS` slams into frame in uppercase tracked-out type.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The tech-spec readout on the hero card ticking through Agency / Logic / Flow / Stack, terminal-style.
- The Service Matrix rows (Brand Identity, Web Development, AI Content Creation, Video & Reels) snapping in one after another like a system scanning its own capabilities.
- The Intake Terminal actually being used: fields fill, service tags light up orange, the package badge locks in, and `TRANSMIT REQUEST →` fires.

## Outro / punchline
The form's own success state lands: a checkmark, `REQUEST TRANSMITTED`, `RESPONSE WITHIN 24 HOURS // AX-PROTOCOL ACTIVE`. Hard cut to the `AX // PBA` mark and `projectsbyaxy.com`. Final line: `Shift your reality.`

## User flow worth showing
The Intake Terminal, start to finish — this is the strongest material in the project because it's real, working product, not marketing copy:
1. **Entry** — Operator Name and Email fields fill in (simulated typing).
2. **Key action** — two service tags (Web Development, AI Content Creation) tick on, the "Domination" package badge auto-locks in response, then `TRANSMIT REQUEST →` is clicked.
3. **Result** — the button's transmitting spinner resolves into the `REQUEST TRANSMITTED` success card.

## Tone
- Preset: `default`
- Creative direction: confident hacker-terminal ops briefing — dense monospace HUD energy, playful and shareable, not a joke at the product's expense.
- Interpretation: quick, clean cuts with a steady upbeat pulse under everything; text slams in fast then holds so the terminal jargon is actually readable; restraint over chaos — this is a agency flexing competence, not a meme.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 19s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#000000`
- Accent: Tailwind `orange-400` (`#fb923c`), used at low opacity for glows/badges/labels
- Text: `#ffffff` (primary), white at reduced opacity for secondary/tertiary copy
- Display font: Space Grotesk (headlines)
- Body font: JetBrains Mono (all labels, specs, UI chrome — uppercase, wide letter-spacing)
- Strongest visual element: the Intake Terminal — black card, orange service-tag checkboxes, monospace field labels, white "TRANSMIT REQUEST →" button

## Share copy (draft)
We didn't just build a contact form. We built a secure transmission protocol for hiring your next dev agency. ⚡ projectsbyaxy.com

## Audio direction
- Role: warm confident bed with tasteful mechanical/interface accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` — upbeat, 120 BPM, business-moves energy
- Music treatment: start at 0s under the hook, hold steady through the middle, brief dip under the transmit-button click, back up for the outro, quick fade over the final logo hold
- Music cue guidance: preset read from `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json` (120.19 BPM). Strong cues at 16.02s, 17.02s, 17.52s, 18.02s, 18.52s, 20.02s — land the "TRANSMIT REQUEST" click and the success-card reveal on the cues nearest 15-17s, and lock the final logo hold near 18-19s. Beat grid (~0.5s spacing, e.g. 6.52, 7.02, 7.52, 8.02...) available for the Service Matrix row-by-row reveal in Scene 3.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the orange glow around the hero "Neural Core" image and the terminal card edges breathe gently with the music's bass/RMS. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, professional restraint
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 1 — logo typing in with soft key-tick sounds
  - Scene 2 — spec rows ticking in with light interface clicks
  - Scene 3 — service rows snapping in one by one, each with a short card sound, timed to the beat grid
  - Scene 4 — checkbox tags ticking on, simulated typing in the name/email fields, a distinct "transmit" whoosh on button click
  - Scene 5 — a clean confirmation chime on the success checkmark, restrained final hit on the logo landing
- Restraint rule: no laser/sci-fi cliché sounds despite the "terminal" theme — sounds should read as real UI (clicks, ticks, soft whooshes), not a movie hacking scene.
