# Brag Plan: ProjectsByAxy (Cinematic Motion-Graphics Edit)

## What is this app?
An AI-powered creative agency site for Indian founders — brand identity, web builds, and content, shipped fast, direct with the founder.

## The angle
A third cut of the launch video, deliberately different in kind from the phonk real-footage edit already on main: this one leans into editorial motion graphics — bold mixed-weight kinetic typography, sharp geometric "shard" accents marking scene changes, and real site content presented as framed devices/cards rather than full-bleed footage — inspired by a user-supplied reference clip's aesthetic (purple/cream editorial motion-design reel: serif-italic-bold + regular type mixes, cutout graphics on flat color blocks, white shard accent shapes, a nested "screen" showing an animated line chart as a visual metaphor). Same technique, translated into ProjectsByAxy's own black/`#fb923c`-orange/Archivo Black+JetBrains Mono identity — not the reference's purple/cream palette.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Hard black. Cold-open mark ignites (reused from prior cuts), then a mixed-weight kinetic line slams in: small mono line "WE DON'T BUILD" over a huge Archivo Black orange "WEBSITES." — same two-tier type hierarchy language as the reference's "stick in / your mind?" beat.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The real hero video (reused from the phonk cut) as the drop — still the strongest "show the thing" beat, now framed with shard accents instead of the vignette-flash treatment.
- A new visual-metaphor scene: an animated SVG line chart with glowing beat-synced dot markers inside a dark inset "screen" card, echoing the reference's data-chart insert, captioned as real outcomes rather than vanity metrics.
- Three real site sections (Services, Process, Projects) presented as framed device cards — orange-bordered rectangle floating on black with a shard accent — rather than full-bleed screenshots, giving the "cutout on a flat background" feel of the reference without inventing illustration assets we don't have.
- A punchline kinetic-type beat ("This Isn't A Template.") echoing the reference's "this isn't Decoration" beat.
- The real intake-terminal fill-up sequence, reused verbatim from the approved phonk cut (zero effects, instant on-beat cuts) — this pattern was explicitly validated by the user and must not be re-decorated with flashes.

## Outro / punchline
Logo + tagline with shard accents, then hard cut to black.

## User flow worth showing
The real intake-terminal fill sequence (entry → fill → submit → success), reused from the prior approved cut.

- Creative direction: editorial motion graphics — kinetic mixed-weight type, geometric shard accents, framed "device card" presentation of real content — in ProjectsByAxy's own black/orange identity, not the reference's purple/cream
- Interpretation: fewer, more confident scenes with dramatic type reveals; real footage stays but is framed/composed rather than full-bleed; shard accents mark every hard cut so transitions read as designed events, not just cuts

## Audio direction
- Role: dense rhythmic layer, same as the two prior cuts
- Music: assets/music/akxneshiva.mp3 — same track as both prior brag videos, beat grid reused from beats/assets/music/akxneshiva.mp3.json (first strong beat 1.357s, ~0.3s/beat grid)
- Music treatment: full volume throughout, matches prior cuts' posture (bed under type/graphics, slightly hotter under the hero-video drop), hard fade on the final cut to black
- Music cue guidance: every scene boundary below lands exactly on a beat-grid multiple of 1.357 + 0.3n; hard hits at cold open, hook slam, hero drop, and outro; softer cuts elsewhere
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — the visual-metaphor chart's dot markers pulse with beat strength
- SFX posture: sparse, same restraint as before — one hit per hard-cut scene, none layered over the terminal sequence's readable text
- Audio-coupled moments: shard-accent snaps synced to the cut; chart dot arrivals synced to sub-beats
- Restraint rule: never flash/shake over the terminal sequence (explicit prior user correction — content must stay legible) or over any scene carrying more than ~3 words of body copy
