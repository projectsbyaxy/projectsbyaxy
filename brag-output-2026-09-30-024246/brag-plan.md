# Brag Plan: ProjectsByAxy — Editorial Reel (reference-matched rebuild)

## Why this rebuild
Previous cuts were rejected as slop: static screenshots, decorative triangles, generic kinetic type. The user supplied a new reference (vertical Instagram "branding secret" reel). This version copies that reference's actual motion system instead of inventing one.

## What the reference does (studied at 0.5s intervals)
- Cream editorial canvas; black curved 4-point-star "swoosh" shapes grow in from opposite corners and keep rotating.
- Small italic setup line types in letter by letter, each letter dropping in slightly raised.
- Huge bold payoff words in one accent color blur in to sharp, line by line, staggered left/right.
- A hero cutout object rises in from below with the payoff text layered *behind* it (the statue's head covers part of "tells").
- Black interstitials: small glowing white line + glowing accent line + blurred accent corner stars.
- Push/whip transitions, floating depth-of-field objects around a device, circular avatar outro.

## Translation to ProjectsByAxy
- Format: vertical 1080x1920 (the reference and the Instagram target are vertical).
- Palette: reference's cream/black system with the brand's orange as the accent — #c2410c on cream (passes contrast), #fb923c glowing on black.
- Type: Archivo italic for setup lines, Archivo Black for payoffs (brand display family).
- Hero cutout: the founder, cut out of the real hero video (visor ignition intact) via `hyperframes remove-background`, rising in with the payoff "websites." layered behind his head.
- Devices: live screencast recordings of the real site (Edge, 1.5–2x) shown inside CSS laptop/phone mockups — hero with cursor hovers, mobile scroll into the Service Matrix, the intake form being filled with a camera push-in (pricing row hidden per the user's earlier instruction; form never submitted).
- Floating service chips with depth-of-field blur around the phone (the reference's floating icons).
- All copy is verbatim site copy: "We don't build websites. We engineer digital ecosystems… for the founders who dare to think differently." / "Our work is measured in conversions, not aesthetics." / "no scope creep, no agency theatre. Just precision execution." / "Shift your reality."
